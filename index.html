04 / Research

Finance beyond the textbook.

Academic work applying financial models to real corporate conditions in Bangladesh.

FIN4707 • Working Capital Management

Empirical Application of the Baumol Model on DSE-Listed Firms

Research examining cash-management efficiency across ACI Formulations PLC, AMCL-PRAN and Golden Harvest Agro Industries over FY2022-23 to FY2024-25.

C* = √(2TF / i)

The study compares theoretical optimal cash balances with actual year-end cash holdings and discusses why real-world Bangladeshi firms may deliberately hold excess liquidity because of precautionary motives, exchange-rate uncertainty, banking frictions, supply-chain risks and operational realities.

Research profile

3DSE-listed firms
3Financial years
6.5–9.4%Opportunity-cost rates used

The project uses audited financial statements, a standardized BDT 10,000 transaction-cost assumption and fiscal-year-specific opportunity-cost rates. It is an academic model—not a claim that actual treasury decisions follow the Baumol optimum mechanically.
