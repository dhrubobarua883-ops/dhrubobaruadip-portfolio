04 / Research/ Academic Project

Research/ Academic Paper

Empirical Application of the Baumol Model on DSE-Listed Firms and Corporate Liquidity Realities in Bangladesh

Working Capital Management · FIN4707

An empirical application of the Baumol Cash Management Model to ACI Formulations PLC, AMCL-PRAN and Golden Harvest Agro Industries to compare theoretical optimal cash balances with actual corporate liquidity.

The research demonstrates a clear gap between theoretical cash optimization and real-world liquidity management. AMCL-PRAN remained closest to its theoretical optimal cash level, while ACI Formulations and Golden Harvest consistently maintained substantially higher cash holdings, reflecting precautionary liquidity, macroeconomic uncertainty, credit constraints and operational realities.

Academic Paper

Financial Weakness and Regulatory-Led Bank Mergers in Bangladesh: Evidence from Selected Islamic Banks

Financial Institutions & Markets · FIN 4703

An empirical banking study examining whether capital, asset-quality, earnings and liquidity weaknesses provide an economic rationale for regulatory-led consolidation of selected Islamic banks in Bangladesh. The research covers FSIBL, SIBL and GIBL using publicly available audited disclosures from 2016-2024.

Uses a CAMELS-based diagnostic to identify asset-quality deterioration, capital erosion, provisioning stress and liquidity fragility. The paper interprets the merger as primarily a resolution/stability mechanism rather than a conventional synergy-driven merger.
